The relaunch's site-level fixes (owner's review of the www and Engineering mocks, 2026-10-30) move into the system, so www and Engineering can drop their overrides when they adopt this version.

- **Header popups close properly (accessibility).** The language dropdown (`HWioLanguageSwitcher`, now `details[data-hwio-dropdown]`) closes on a click outside, on Escape (focus back on its summary), when focus leaves it and when another popup opens; opening it closes an open mega panel. HWioHeader's mega panels and drawer join the same one-popup-at-a-time protocol (`hwio:popupopen`, new `@hubpav/hwio-web-runtime/popup`). New runtime `/dropdown`. Also fixed: on hover devices the click that followed a hover-open closed the mega panel again; it now keeps it open, the next click closes it.
- **Quiet header utilities.** HWioHeader's theme toggle and language switcher carry `.hwio-utility`: the nav's weight (Regular), 15 px (14 px with `navDensity="tight"` from 1280 to 1535 px), 80 % ink, the nav hover colour, the toggle icon at 18 px, the chevron at half opacity. A CTA with an icon (the store) collapses to the icon alone below 2xl; its external glyph now shows only beside the full label (`HWioButton` gains `externalIcon`).
- **Menu panels.** New `HWioMegaLinks` for `mega:<key>` slots: caps group title in Gray, an optional "all" link, labels that never wrap, descriptions 13 px Regular Gray balanced (`.hwio-menu-desc`), optional product thumbnails with the product shadow, and a fixed column count (no switch to three columns at 2xl). The drawer's second lines use `.hwio-menu-desc` too (were 12 px at 70 % opacity).
- **Footer bottom line.** Copyright and tagline centred together with a middle dot between them; stacked and centred without the dot on phones.
- **Fail-safe motion.** Scroll reveal hides content only after the reveal runtime has started (`html.hwio-reveal-on`; was `.js`, set before paint, so a failed script left sections invisible). Hiding is instant, revealing eases in once; `data-hwio-reveal-delay` staggers (applied through the CSSOM); `--hwio-reveal-duration` and `--hwio-reveal-travel` calm it per page; threshold 0 so tall sections reveal. New `HWioReveal` starts it (renders nothing). Count-up per figure (`data-hwio-count`, `-from`, `-template`, `-group`) ending on the exact server text, pure logic in `/count-core` with tests; the stats band's `data-hwio-counter` is unchanged. Hero entrance `.hwio-enter` / `.hwio-enter-media` (CSS only, after the font gate; HWioHeroSplit `enter`). Arrow nudge `.hwio-arrow` (HWioButton's arrow carries it). New runtime `/iso` and component `HWioIsoScene`: an isometric scene from the Brand Guidelines' kit builds itself once in view (`.iso-object`, `.iso-trace`, `.iso-pad`, `.iso-radio`), with the kit's dark-theme colours. Everything shows its final state under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Components that recur.** `HWioKeyFigure` (the one gradient key figure, optional count-up), `HWioStoryCard` (field photograph under the guidelines' scrim, caps kicker, linked card), `HWioApplicationTile` (photo or cut-out, row on phones, optional references and reveal), `HWioCutout` (product cut-out with the one shadow recipe, common baseline and floor, Panel or plain ground, optional isometric backdrop), `HWioPhoto`. New classes `.hwio-product-shadow`, `.hwio-product-floor`, `.hwio-cutout-ground`, `.hwio-backdrop*`, `.hwio-scrim`, `.hwio-card-link` / `.hwio-card-title`, `.hwio-role-heading|subheading|title`, `.hwio-key-figure`.
- **Change requests, opt-in.** `.hwio-grad` (CR-8: up to two key words of a hero H1 in the figure gradient instead of a key figure) and `.hwio-h1-long` / HWioHeroSplit `titleSize="long"` (CR-7: a long H1 at 36 / 44 / 48 / 52 px) are pending the Brand Guidelines. Gradient type now clears the fill (`-webkit-text-fill-color`), not `color`, so a theme without the gradient (er3o) shows plain text.
